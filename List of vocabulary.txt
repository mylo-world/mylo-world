List of vocabulary
# Academic Terms
thesis: A statement or theory put forward to be maintained or proved.
paradigm: A typical example or pattern of something; a model.
synthesis: The combination of ideas to form a theory or system.
methodology: A system of methods used in a particular area of study or activity.
epistemology: The theory of knowledge, especially with regard to its methods, validity, and scope.
pedagogy: The method and practice of teaching, especially as an academic subject or theoretical concept.
dissertation: A long essay on a particular subject, especially one written for a university degree or diploma.
hypothesis: A proposed explanation made based on limited evidence as a starting point for further investigation.
lexicon: The vocabulary of a person, language, or branch of knowledge.
colloquium: An academic conference or seminar.

# Research and Study
citation: A reference to a published or unpublished source.
abstract: A summary of a research article, thesis, review, or other scientific document.
empirical: Based on observation or experience rather than theory or pure logic.
qualitative: Relating to, measuring, or measured by the quality of something rather than its quantity.
quantitative: Relating to, measuring, or measured by the quantity of something rather than its quality.
bibliography: A list of the books referred to in a scholarly work.
data analysis: The process of inspecting, cleansing, transforming, and modeling data with the goal of discovering useful information.
literature review: A survey of scholarly sources on a specific topic.
longitudinal study: Research that involves repeated observations of the same variables over long periods of time.
variable: An element, feature, or factor that is liable to vary or change.

# Tech and Computer Science
algorithm: A process or set of rules to be followed in calculations or other problem-solving operations.
cybersecurity: The practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.
database: An organized collection of structured information or data, typically stored electronically in a computer system.
encryption: The process of converting information or data into a code to prevent unauthorized access.
interface: A point where two systems, subjects, organizations, etc., meet and interact.
machine learning: A type of artificial intelligence that allows software applications to become more accurate at predicting outcomes.
network: A group of two or more computer systems linked together.
open source: Software for which the original source code is made freely available and may be redistributed and modified.
compiler: A program that translates code written in a high-level programming language into machine code that can be executed by a computer's CPU.
artificial intelligence: The simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems.

# Natural Science
isotope: Variants of a particular chemical element that differ in neutron number, and consequently in nucleon number.
photosynthesis: The process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize foods with the help of chlorophyll.
ecosystem: A biological community of interacting organisms and their physical environment.
kinetics: The study of the rates at which chemical processes occur.
evolution: The process by which different kinds of living organisms are thought to have developed from earlier forms during the history of the earth.
biodiversity: The variety of plant and animal life in the world or in a particular habitat.
osmosis: The process of gradual or unconscious assimilation of ideas, knowledge, etc.
geology: The science that deals with the earth's physical structure and substance.
cell: The smallest structural and functional unit of an organism.
microbiology: The branch of science that deals with microorganisms.

# Humanities and Social Science
anthropology: The study of human societies and cultures and their development.
sociology: The study of the development, structure, and functioning of human society.
semantics: The branch of linguistics and logic concerned with meaning.
hermeneutics: The branch of knowledge that deals with interpretation, especially of the Bible or literary texts.
dialectic: The art of investigating or discussing the truth of opinions.
ethics: The branch of knowledge that deals with moral principles.
philosophy: The study of the fundamental nature of knowledge, reality, and existence.
linguistics: The scientific study of language and its structure.
political science: The branch of knowledge that deals with systems of government and the analysis of political activity and behavior.
socioeconomics: The study of the relationship between economic activity and social life.

# Business and Economics
market segmentation: The process of dividing a market of potential customers into groups, or segments, based on different characteristics.
supply chain: The sequence of processes involved in the production and distribution of a commodity.
fiscal policy: The use of government revenue collection and expenditure to influence the economy.
capitalism: An economic system in which a country's trade and industry are controlled by private owners for profit, rather than by the state.
innovation: The action or process of innovating; introducing new ideas, methods, or products.
monopoly: The exclusive possession or control of the supply or trade in a commodity or service.
inflation: A general increase in prices and fall in the purchasing value of money.
entrepreneurship: The activity of setting up a business or businesses, taking on financial risks in the hope of profit.
gross domestic product: The total value of goods produced and services provided in a country during one year.
market analysis: The study of the attractiveness and the dynamics of a special market within a special industry.

# Engineering
circuit: A closed path through which an electric current flows or may flow.
thermodynamics: The branch of physical science that deals with the relations between heat and other forms of energy.
structural analysis: The determination of the effects of loads on physical structures and their components.
robotics: The branch of technology that deals with the design, construction, operation, and application of robots.
nanotechnology: The branch of technology that deals with dimensions and tolerances of less than 100 nanometers, especially the manipulation of individual atoms and molecules.
mechanics: The branch of applied mathematics dealing with motion and forces producing motion.
aerospace: The branch of technology and industry concerned with both aviation and space flight.
biomaterials: Any matter, surface, or construct that interacts with biological systems.
renewable energy: Energy from a source that is not depleted when used, such as wind or solar power.
ergonomics: The study of people's efficiency in their working environment.

# Health and Medicine
pathology: The science of the causes and effects of diseases.
pharmacology: The branch of medicine concerned with the uses, effects, and modes of action of drugs.
anatomy: The branch of science concerned with the bodily structure of humans, animals, and other living organisms.
epidemiology: The branch of medicine which deals with the incidence, distribution, and control of diseases.
immunology: The branch of medicine and biology concerned with immunity.
genetics: The study of heredity and the variation of inherited characteristics.
cardiology: The branch of medicine that deals with diseases and abnormalities of the heart.
oncology: The study and treatment of tumors.
psychiatry: The study and treatment of mental illness, emotional disturbance, and abnormal behavior.
surgery: The branch of medical practice that treats injuries, deformities, or other disorders by manual operation or instrumental appliances.
